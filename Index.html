About Us

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit organization working across India to support education, food distribution, animal welfare, women empowerment, environmental sustainability, and skill development initiatives.

Since its establishment, InAmigos Foundation has impacted thousands of lives through various community-driven projects and volunteer-led initiatives. The organization believes that collective efforts can create meaningful and lasting change in society.

Recognized & Registered

Section 8 NGO 80G 12A CSR-1 NITI Aayog

Mission

Empower communities through education, social welfare, environmental sustainability, and skill development initiatives.

Vision

Build a compassionate and self-reliant society where every individual has access to opportunities, dignity, and support.

Our Impact

Transforming lives through Project Seva, Bachpanshala, Jeev, Udaan, Prakriti, and Vikas by addressing hunger, education, animal welfare, women empowerment, environmental conservation, and employability development.

Why Our Work Matters

Education for Every Child

Education is the foundation of a better future, yet millions of children across India still face barriers to quality learning opportunities. Poverty, lack of resources, and limited access to technology prevent many students from reaching their full potential. Through Project Bachpanshala, InAmigos Foundation works to bridge these gaps by providing educational support, digital literacy training, life skills development, and learning opportunities that empower children to build brighter futures.

Hunger Relief & Community Support

Access to nutritious food remains a challenge for many underprivileged families. Hunger affects health, education, productivity, and overall well-being. Through Project Seva, InAmigos Foundation distributes meals, clothing, and essential supplies to vulnerable communities. By supporting individuals and families during difficult times, we aim to create a society where no one is left behind and everyone has the opportunity to live with dignity.

Environmental Sustainability

Climate change and environmental degradation impact communities across the world. Sustainable actions today can protect future generations from serious ecological challenges. Through Project Prakriti, InAmigos Foundation promotes environmental awareness, tree plantation drives, and eco-friendly practices. With thousands of saplings planted and community participation encouraged, we strive to create greener, healthier, and more sustainable surroundings.

Women Empowerment

Empowered women contribute to stronger families, communities, and economies. However, many women still face social, educational, and financial challenges that limit their opportunities. Through Project Udaan, InAmigos Foundation supports women through skill development programs, awareness campaigns, self-help group collaborations, and financial independence initiatives. Our goal is to help women gain confidence, achieve self-reliance, and become active leaders within their communities.

Animal Welfare

Animals are an important part of our ecosystem and deserve care, protection, and compassion. Many stray animals struggle daily with hunger, injury, and lack of shelter. Through Project Jeev, InAmigos Foundation works toward animal welfare by supporting feeding programs, rescue efforts, and awareness activities that encourage responsible and humane treatment of animals. Every act of kindness helps create a more compassionate society.

Skill Development & Employment

Skills create opportunities, and opportunities transform lives. In today's rapidly changing world, practical knowledge and professional skills are essential for career growth. Through Project Vikas, InAmigos Foundation provides internship opportunities, training programs, and professional development support across various fields. By helping young individuals gain experience and confidence, we prepare them for successful careers and long-term economic independence.
